What we build

Trading Platform Capabilities

The parts a trading platform needs to actually go live.

Integration With Brokers
We help select and integrate brokers based on market coverage, API quality, latency, and SLA.
Trading Execution Middleware
Messaging, market-data fan-out, FIX and proprietary protocols in one low-latency layer to connect services across the trading chain.
Market Data Storage
A market database pulling multiple sources via APIs, with historical intraday data for equities, futures, FX, options, indices, and indicators.
Other Features
Extend broker capabilities with portfolio management, FIX gateways, and scalable matching components.
Order Management
Manage order types (market, stop, limit, etc.), define quantity, leverage, time in force, and price, and execute stop-loss and take-profit strategies.
Trading Algorithms
We implement your strategy and provide a no-code builder with parameter checks and safeguards.
Strategy Builder
Both a visual builder and a code editor, so your users can create their own strategies with different indicators and algorithms.
Backtesting
Test and optimize strategies using historical market data before risking money on a real trade.
Investor Interfaces
Web, mobile, and desktop interfaces with fast presets, hotkeys, and watchlists.
Real-Time Alerts on Changes
Fine-tuned asset alerts notify users within seconds when a target price for one of their assets or exchange-traded funds rises or falls.
Trade Log
Detailed trading histories (rules set for orders, instruments, order types, number of contracts, price, and time) make it possible to identify mistakes and adjust strategies.
Paper Trading
Simulated trades under real market conditions let users test strategies and practice buying and selling without risking real money.

FAQs

How much does trading platform development cost?

Cost depends on the engagement model. Efficient Hours (Agile with Budget Control) bills only efficient hours against an agreed estimate. Fixed Price sets the full cost upfront once requirements are documented. Outstaffing bills a pre-agreed monthly rate per engineer. See the Engagement Models section above.

How long does it take to build a trading platform?

Trading platform software development usually takes about 3 months or more, and directly depends on the features you want to implement. Get in touch to find out the estimated development time for a project like yours.

Can you create a trading platform and integrate it with a third-party service?

Yes, we work with world-class service providers and integrate third-party APIs and services according to your business goals: brokers, payment gateways, KYC providers, news providers, market data providers, crypto exchanges, and more.

Do you offer support services?

Yes, we offer post-production support on a regular basis or on demand.

Do you sign an NDA agreement?

We are comfortable signing legal agreements when developing a trading platform. We can either sign your NDA contract or create our own and send it to you.

Have you built a trading platform before?

Yes, we have extensive experience in trading software development, with a specialty in stock market application development. See our trading platform case studies above.

I have an idea for a trading platform. Where should I start?

Your next step depends on whether you have a clear product vision and detailed requirements. If not, we recommend that you start with the discovery phase. Contact us to discuss your idea.

How to hire trading platform developers?

Define your project scope and tech stack, then search for candidates with experience in financial software, security protocols, and relevant technologies. Assess candidates through technical interviews and coding tests, and prioritize a proven track record in developing secure, scalable trading platforms.
